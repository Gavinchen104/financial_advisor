NVIDIA

NVDA
+4.2% · past 7 days
Educational example · not live data

What happened

NVIDIA closed the week up 4.2%, touching a fresh intraday high after announcing deeper AI-chip partnerships with three major cloud providers at its GTC developer conference.

Why the price moved

Management raised data-center revenue guidance for the coming quarter, citing continued demand for its Blackwell-generation GPUs from hyperscalers (large cloud providers like AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud) [1]. Most analysts responded with upward price-target revisions, though a few flagged valuation concerns at a forward P/E (price-to-earnings ratio based on expected future profits) near 36 [2].

What it means for a first-year investor

Moves like this — several percent in a week on forward-looking guidance — are typical for large-cap names at the center of a major theme like AI. The stock's day-to-day price tends to track investor expectations about future AI-hardware demand more than today's earnings number.
